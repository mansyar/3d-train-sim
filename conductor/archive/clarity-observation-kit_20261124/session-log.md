Complete this only from a real, consented session. Every result starts `not measured`. Do not enter estimates, child names, or identities.

## Privacy and consent

- Consent was given before the session: [ ] yes  [ ] no. If no, stop and leave every row `not measured`.
- If consent is withdrawn, delete the notes and mark the row `not measured`.
