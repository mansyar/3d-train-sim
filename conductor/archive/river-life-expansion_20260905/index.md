## Summary

The meadow's river comes alive: a chunky cargo barge drifts the S-curve
day in, day out — passing under the trestle bridges, pausing at night,
and sitting frozen in the winter ice — while frogs on lily pads join the
critter tab as the one scenery piece that may float on the water. Frogs
hop and ribbit (mute-respecting) when a train passes, riding the
existing critter-life pattern. Purely additive: older saved railways
open unchanged, no save-version bump.
