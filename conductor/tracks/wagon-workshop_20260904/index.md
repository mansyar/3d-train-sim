## Summary

Every locomotive pulls two bundled cargo wagons (lumber + box) with no
choice. Wagon Workshop unlocks the unused Kenney kit wagon variety
(coal, tank, container, flatbed, colors) behind an icon-only picker so
kids can dress their freight — same autonomous ride, same cargo
gameplay, just THEIR train. DRAFT spec — to be refined interactively
before planning.
