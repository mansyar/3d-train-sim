# Clarity Quick Card

Use this card during one real session. Leave every result `not measured` until you actually see it happen. This card is a timing aid, not a test and not a score.

## Before you start

- Ask the caregiver to consent. If consent is withdrawn, stop, delete the notes, and mark the row `not measured`.
- Use the real family device in its normal setting. Note only: iPad Safari or Android Chrome; PWA or browser tab; starting state; app open or freshly woken.
- No names, no photos, no video, no audio. Keep notes anonymous.
- Put the log sheet beside you. Record interruptions as interruptions; do not adjust a clock to make a session look smoother.

## Clock 1 — time to first ride

- **Start:** the moment the app is opened or the device wakes into the already-open app.
- **Stop:** the child completes the first self-directed ride. Self-directed means the child chooses and acts without an adult prompt.
- **Cap:** stop at 20:00 even if no ride has happened. If an adult prompts or helps, stop this clock and write an interruption note.

## Clock 2 — unassisted session length

- **Start:** the child's first self-directed placement.
- **Stop:** the child disengages from the app or an adult gives help.
- **Cap:** stop at 20:00 even if the child is still playing.

## Session flow

1. Confirm consent and note the device/browser and starting state.
2. Start Clock 1 when the app is opened or the device wakes.
3. Watch without prompting. Stop Clock 1 at the first self-directed ride.
4. Start Clock 2 at the first self-directed placement.
5. Stop Clock 2 when the child disengages, help is given, or 20:00 is reached.
6. Write only what you saw in the log. Leave unobserved cells `not measured`.
7. Rank friction by affected-child count then severity. Never invent a rank.

## If something goes wrong

- **Sibling interruption:** note it, pause the observation, and do not treat the interruption as disengagement.
- **Device fails or the app closes:** note it. The affected measure stays `not measured` unless a clean clock was already stopped.
- **No ride within the cap:** stop Clock 1 at 20:00 and mark the measure `not measured (cap reached)`.
- **Cap reached during play:** stop Clock 2 at 20:00 and note that the session was capped.
- **Observers disagree:** both mark the measure `not measured` and write the disagreement. Do not average.

## Human-only device-feel prompts

These are parent notes, not measured clarity values and not agent-claimed checks. Give no pass/fail number.

- iPad Safari: were touch targets easy to hit? Did audio unlock? Did motion feel smooth?
- Android Chrome: were touch targets easy to hit? Did audio unlock? Did motion feel smooth?
