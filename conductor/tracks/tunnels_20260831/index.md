## Summary

The meadow gains a tunnel: a chunky grassy dome with a rounded arch the
train rides through. A new `tunnel` track piece type (rides like the
straight it mirrors, dry-land-only terrain rule) that kids drag from the
toybox; adjacent tunnels merge into continuous long tunnels with portals
only at the run's ends; the train disappears inside and pops out the far
side. Delight extras in V1: whistle echo + chug duck inside, a snow cap in
winter, and the headlight catching the portals at night. Asset is a
Blender-authored GLB with named nodes (dome, portal arches, snow cap),
measured to the straight kit's mount like the trestle bridge. Purely
additive to the save schema — old worlds load untouched.
