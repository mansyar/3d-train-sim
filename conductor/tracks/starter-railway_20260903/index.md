## Summary

Every first boot lands on a rideable Cozy Oval (closed loop + station,
`steam` loco) instead of empty grass, and the long-press parent gate gains
an icon-only 3-preset gallery (Cozy Oval, Station Village, River Crossing)
for reloading a starter after chaos. Starters are ordinary worlds —
autosaved, editable, trashable — and gallery picks replace as one
single-undoable mutation. No save bump, no new assets, no new sounds.
